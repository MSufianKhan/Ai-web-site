Our story

Smoky Stack started with one idea: burgers and pizza should taste like someone cared. Everything is cooked to order, from hand-stretched dough to smashed-to-order patties.

Made fresh,
served fast

Our dough is proofed for 48 hours, our patties are ground daily and our vegetables arrive every morning from local farms. Nothing sits under a heat lamp.

Come in for a quick bite or stay for the whole evening. Our team keeps the tables clean, the music low and the food hot.
